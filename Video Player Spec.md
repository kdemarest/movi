# Video Player Spec

Handles its own pre-caching and cache emptying.

Capabilities:
- pre-caching
- layers
- timeline

Layer Ops
- movement from a to b with functions like ease in, ease out
- scaling around a point
- rotation around an arbitrary point
- fade/translucency
- maybe saturation, brightness and contrast

Camera Ops
- shake amount, duration. Amount can be a function, like a sine wave
- zoom to point
- pan point to point
- scene wipes, fades, or zooms
- flicker or raster scan lines
